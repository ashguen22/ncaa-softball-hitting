College Softball’s Best Lineups (2021–2022)

Exploring Powerhouses, Speed Offenses, and All 479 Division I Team-Seasons

One superstar can change an inning, but winning a conference or reaching Oklahoma City takes an entire batting order. Across 479 full NCAA Division I team-seasons in 2021 and 2022 (programs with at least 7 qualifying hitters in the dataset), the typical college lineup posted an .802 weighted OPS—combining a .363 On-Base Percentage (OBP) with a .439 Slugging Percentage (SLG).

Yet colleges built dangerous lineups using very different blueprints:

  • 100-Homer Juggernauts: Only four lineups cracked 100 home runs in a single season—2021 Oklahoma (154), 2022 Oklahoma (149), 2022 Wichita State (111), and 2022 Arkansas (103).
  • Balanced Five-Tool Offenses: Programs like 2022 Duke (82 HRs, 74 SBs), 2022 UL Lafayette (69 HRs, 80 SBs), and 2021 Miami (OH) (74 HRs, 71 SBs) reached the national Top 15 by pairing over-the-fence power with relentless base stealing.
  • Track-Meet Pressure: Southeastern Louisiana stole 142 bases in 2021 and 133 in 2022—climbing to a .912 team OPS in 2022 despite hitting only 31 home runs—alongside speed-first contenders like 2022 Florida (126 SBs) and 2022 McNeese (128 SBs).
